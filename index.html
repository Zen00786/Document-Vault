Know before you begin

PIN and recovery key

Your PIN is the everyday way to unlock the vault on this device. Choose one you can remember, and don't share it.

The recovery key may be required to regain access or restore your encrypted vault, depending on the recovery flow in your app version. Keep it private and store it somewhere safe, separate from your device.

If you lose access to both your PIN and the required recovery information, encrypted documents may become permanently inaccessible. Recovery depends on the app's actual recovery mechanism.

⌑

A little preparation matters.

Save the recovery key when the app offers it. Don't store it only inside the vault it is meant to help recover.
